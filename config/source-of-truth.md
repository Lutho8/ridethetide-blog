## 2. Canonical Writing Voice

### Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Description | Do | Don't |
|-----------|-------------|-----|-------|
| **Scientific but accessible** | Grounded in research, never condescending | Cite studies, explain mechanisms simply | Use jargon without explanation, dumb it down |
| **Direct and honest** | No hype, no fear-mongering | State benefits AND limitations | Hype like "miracle cure," scare like "you're doing it wrong" |
| **South African context** | Local references, local regulations, local suppliers | Mention SA-specific laws, CPT/JHB references | Pretend we're in the US or EU |
| **Practitioner-first** | Written by someone who actually uses peptides | Share personal protocols, timing, experiences | Write like a marketer who has never reconstituted a vial |
| **Safety-obsessed** | Every article mentions risks, contraindications, need for medical supervision | Lead with safety, include disclaimer | Gloss over side effects, ignore contraindications |

### Reference Articles (Tone Benchmarks)

1. **"The Complete BPC-157 Guide for South African Researchers"** — Deep-dive, mechanism-first, dosing tables, SA supplier notes, explicit safety warnings
2. **"How I Lost 8kg in 6 Weeks: A Protocol Breakdown"** — First-person, data-driven, honest about struggles, weekly timeline
3. **"Peptide Reconstitution: The Math Nobody Teaches You"** — Tutorial-style, step-by-step photos, common mistakes highlighted
4. **"Why Most Peptide 'Stacks' Are Dangerous"** — Contrarian, evidence-based, calls out bro-science, offers safer alternatives

### Prohibited Phrases

- "Miracle peptide"
- "Doctors hate this"
- "Biohack your way to..."
- "Game-changer" (unless quoting a study)
- "Secret the industry doesn't want you to know"
- Any claim of FDA approval for research peptides

### Required Elements in Every Article

1. Medical disclaimer in first 100 words
2. At least one primary research citation
3. South African regulatory context (SAHPRA, scheduling)
4. Dosing information must include: compound, amount, frequency, duration, route
5. Contraindications and interaction warnings
6. "Talk to your doctor" callout

## 6. Content Governance

### Publishing Workflow

1. **Seed** — Topic identified via gap analysis / customer language / trend
2. **Research** — Primary sources gathered, citations verified
3. **Draft** — AI-assisted first draft using voice guidelines
4. **Review** — Human review for accuracy, safety, tone
5. **Legal** — Medical disclaimer verified, claims checked
6. **Publish** — SEO optimization, internal linking, schema markup
7. **Distribute** — Social, email, community, AI directories
8. **Measure** — Rankings, traffic, engagement, conversions
9. **Refresh** — Quarterly update cycle for top articles

### Content Refresh Triggers

- Traffic decline >20% for 2 consecutive months
- New research published contradicting existing claims
- Regulatory change in SA
- Competitor publishes superior content on same topic
- AI search visibility drop flagged by Brand Radar
- User feedback / correction submitted
