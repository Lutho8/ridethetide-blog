## What Is CJC-1295 (No DAC)?

CJC-1295 without DAC is technically known as **Modified GRF 1-29** (Mod GRF 1-29). It is a 29-amino acid analogue of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) with four amino acid substitutions (Tyr1, Ala8, Asn15, Leu27) that increase its stability and binding affinity to the GHRH receptor on somatotroph cells in the anterior pituitary.

### Mechanism of Action

When CJC-1295 (no DAC) binds to the GHRH receptor, it stimulates the pulsatile release of growth hormone (GH) from the pituitary. The "no DAC" distinction is critical:

- **Without DAC:** The peptide has a short half-life (~30 minutes). It creates a brief, physiologic pulse of GH that mimics natural secretion patterns. This pulsatility preserves negative feedback loops and reduces the risk of GH desensitisation and insulin resistance [1].
- **With DAC:** The drug affinity complex (DAC) binds to albumin, extending the half-life to several days. This creates a constant, elevated GH level that is not physiologic and has been associated with increased risk of insulin resistance, water retention, and potential pituitary desensitisation [2].

**For research purposes, CJC-1295 without DAC is the only version we recommend.** The "with DAC" variant may produce higher IGF-1 numbers on paper, but the cost-benefit profile skews unfavourably for long-term health.

## What Is Ipamorelin?

Ipamorelin is a **selective growth hormone secretagogue** (GHS) and a ghrelin receptor agonist. Unlike older GHRPs (GHRP-6, GHRP-2), Ipamorelin was designed to stimulate GH release with minimal off-target effects.

### Selectivity Profile

| Hormone | GHRP-6 Elevation | GHRP-2 Elevation | Ipamorelin Elevation |
|---------|-----------------|------------------|---------------------|
| Growth hormone | Strong | Strong | Strong |
| Cortisol | Moderate | Mild | Minimal |
| Prolactin | Moderate | Mild | Minimal |
| Acetylcholine | None | None | None |

This selectivity matters. Elevated cortisol and prolactin are not benign side effects — they contribute to water retention, mood disturbance, fat storage, and (in the case of prolactin) gynecomastia risk in men. Ipamorelin's clean profile makes it the preferred GH secretagogue for researchers who prioritise safety and tolerability [3].

### Mechanism

Ipamorelin binds to the ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a) on somatotroph cells, triggering GH release through a separate pathway from CJC-1295. This is the foundation of the stack's synergy: two different receptors, two different signalling pathways, one outcome — a larger, more sustained GH pulse than either peptide alone.

## The Synergy: Why Stack Them?

CJC-1295 and Ipamorelin are not merely additive. They are synergistic.

### The Amplification Effect

CJC-1295 (a GHRH analogue) increases the **number** of somatotroph cells ready to release GH. Ipamorelin (a ghrelin agonist) increases the **amplitude** of the GH pulse from those primed cells. Together, they produce a GH pulse that is substantially larger than the sum of individual doses [4].

In practical terms:
- CJC-1295 alone: modest GH pulse, short duration.
- Ipamorelin alone: moderate GH pulse, good selectivity.
- **CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin: large GH pulse, sustained release, minimal off-target hormone elevation.**

This synergy allows lower individual doses to achieve superior results, reducing cost and side effect burden.

### Physiologic vs Pharmacologic GH

Exogenous HGH (somatropin) creates a flat, elevated GH level that bypasses the pituitary entirely. Over time, this suppresses natural GH production, raises IGF-1 continuously, and increases insulin resistance risk.

The CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin stack preserves the **pulsatile, nocturnal pattern** of natural GH secretion. Your pituitary remains active. Your feedback loops remain intact. This is the closest pharmacologic approximation to youthful GH physiology currently available [5].

### Injection Timing Options

| Protocol | Timing | Rationale | Best For |
|----------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Once daily** | Before bed (21:00–22:00) | Maximises nocturnal GH pulse, improves sleep | Beginners, cost-conscious |
| **Twice daily** | Morning (fasted) + before bed | Two GH pulses, better fat loss | Intermediate researchers |
| **Three daily** | Morning + post-workout + before bed | Maximises GH exposure, best recovery | Advanced, athletes |

### Practical Co-Injection

Both peptides can be drawn into the same insulin syringe and injected together. This reduces injection frequency and ensures simultaneous receptor activation.

**Example co-injection (bedtime):**
1. Draw 0.1 mL (10 units) of reconstituted CJC-1295 (100 mcg)
2. Draw 0.1 mL (10 units) of reconstituted Ipamorelin (100 mcg)
3. Inject subcutaneously into abdominal fat or deltoid fat pad
4. Do not eat for 30 minutes post-injection (carbohydrates blunt GH release)

Store reconstituted vials refrigerated (2–8°C). Use within 30 days. Do not freeze reconstituted peptides.

## Side Effects & Contraindications

### Common Side Effects (Research Context)

| Side Effect | Frequency | Severity | Management |
|-------------|-----------|----------|------------|
| Injection site irritation | Common | Mild | Rotate sites, use fresh needles |
| Water retention | Common | Mild–Moderate | Self-limiting; reduce sodium; usually resolves week 2–3 |
| Numbness/tingling (hands) | Uncommon | Mild | Carpal tunnel-like; reduce dose if persistent |
| Increased hunger | Uncommon | Mild | Usually transient; adjust meal timing |
| Fatigue (daytime) | Rare | Mild | May indicate excessive dose; reduce or move to bedtime only |
| Headache | Rare | Mild | Hydration, reduce dose |
| Flushing/warmth | Rare | Mild | Transient, post-injection |

### Serious Risks (Rare but Real)

| Risk | Mechanism | Red Flag |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Insulin resistance | Chronic GH elevation impairs glucose uptake | Fasting glucose >6.5 mmol/L, HbA1c rising |
| Pituitary desensitisation | Overstimulation suppresses natural GH | IGF-1 dropping mid-cycle despite continued use |
| Prolactin elevation | Not typical with Ipamorelin, but possible with contamination | Gynecomastia, libido loss, nipple sensitivity |
| Hypothyroidism | GH increases T4-to-T3 conversion demand | Rising TSH, falling fT3, fatigue, cold intolerance |

### Contraindications — Do Not Use If

- Active cancer or history of malignancy (GH/IGF-1 can promote tumour growth)
- Diabetes or pre-diabetes with poor control (HbA1c >7.0%)
- Pituitary adenoma or history of pituitary surgery
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding
- Under 25 years old (GH axis still developing)
- Uncontrolled hypertension
- Severe sleep apnoea (GH can worsen it)

### Why Not GHRP-6 or GHRP-2?

GHRP-6 and GHRP-2 are effective but dirty. They stimulate cortisol and prolactin release alongside GH, and GHRP-6 causes ravenous hunger that sabotages diet compliance for many researchers. They are also more likely to cause water retention and mood disturbance. For most South African researchers, the marginal increase in GH output is not worth the side effect burden.

### Why Not Sermorelin Alone?

Sermorelin is a GHRH analogue (like CJC-1295) but with a shorter half-life and lower potency. It is safer and more natural but produces a weaker GH pulse. For younger researchers (<35) or those with mild GH deficiency, Sermorelin may be sufficient. For maximal physiologic GH enhancement, the CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin stack is superior.

### Why Not Exogenous HGH?

Pharmaceutical HGH is effective but carries significant downsides:
- Suppresses natural pituitary GH production
- Flat GH curve increases insulin resistance risk
- Much more expensive (R8,000–15,000+ for 12 weeks)
- Illegal to possess without prescription in South Africa
- Higher risk of acromegaly features with long-term use

For research purposes, the secretagogue stack is safer, cheaper, and more physiologic.

### Why Not MK-677?

MK-677 is an oral ghrelin agonist with a 24-hour half-life. It is convenient but causes significant hunger, water retention, and potential insulin resistance due to constant receptor activation. It also requires daily dosing without breaks, making it harder to cycle. The injectable CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin stack offers better control, cleaner selectivity, and easier cycling.

## South African Context

### Regulatory Status

CJC-1295 and Ipamorelin are **not scheduled** by SAHPRA and are **not approved for human use**. They fall into the same regulatory grey area as most research peptides: legal to possess for research purposes, illegal to market for human consumption, and unavailable through conventional pharmacies.

**Practical implication:** You will not find these at Dis-Chem or Clicks. They are sourced through international research chemical suppliers or local compounding pharmacies willing to source on request.

### Quality Verification

Before purchasing any peptide, demand:
- **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** from an independent third-party lab (HPLC purity ≥98%)
- **Mass spectrometry confirmation** (confirms molecular identity)
- **Endotoxin testing** (<0.01 EU/μg)
- **Lyophilised appearance** (fluffy white cake, not crushed powder or discoloured)

**Red flags:** No COA available, prices significantly below market (too cheap to be real), supplier refuses third-party testing, reviews that seem fake or paid.

### Shipping & Customs

- Importing small quantities for personal research is generally tolerated
- Always declare as "research chemicals" or "laboratory reagents"
- Expect 7–21 day shipping from North American or European suppliers
- Use tracked shipping; uninsured parcels frequently disappear
- Refrigerate immediately upon receipt

### Storage in South African Climate

South African summers are harsh on peptides. Reconstituted peptides degrade rapidly above 8°C.

- **Lyophilised (powder):** Store in freezer (-20°C) for long-term stability. Use airtight container with desiccant.
- **Reconstituted:** Refrigerate at 2–8°C immediately. Do not leave unrefrigerated during load shedding — use a cooler bag with ice bricks if necessary.
- **Transport:** When travelling, use an insulated medical cooler bag. Peptides can tolerate brief room temperature exposure (2–4 hours) but not repeated warming.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use CJC-1295 with DAC instead?**

You can, but we do not recommend it. CJC-1295 with DAC creates supraphysiologic GH levels that increase insulin resistance risk and may cause pituitary desensitisation. The "no DAC" variant is safer, more natural, and sufficient for research purposes.

**Can I stack this with BPC-157 or TB-500?**

Yes. These stacks are orthogonal — BPC-157 and TB-500 target tissue repair through different mechanisms and do not interfere with GH secretion. Many researchers run healing stacks alongside GH secretagogues.

**Will this show up on a drug test?**

Standard employment drug tests do not screen for peptides. WADA bans GH secretagogues for competitive athletes. If you are a tested athlete, do not use this stack.

**Can women use this stack?**

Yes. The mechanism is identical in women. Dosing does not need adjustment. Women should be particularly vigilant about monitoring prolactin, although Ipamorelin rarely elevates it.

**What if I miss a dose?**

Do not double up. Resume your normal schedule at the next planned injection. Consistency matters more than perfection.
