## Introduction: Why I'm Sharing This

I'm sharing my personal protocol and results for one reason: **transparency.**

The peptide space is flooded with before-and-after photos, miracle claims, and bro-science. Most of it is unverifiable, exaggerated, or outright fabricated. I'm a 38-year-old male, living in Cape Town, with a full-time job, two kids, and a history of yo-yo dieting. I tracked everything — weight, body composition, sleep, energy, mood, blood markers — and I'm going to show you the unvarnished truth.

**Medical disclaimer:** This is a personal research account, not medical advice. I worked with a physician throughout this protocol. Do not attempt without medical supervision.

## The Protocol: Design and Rationale

### Peptide Stack

| Peptide | Dose | Frequency | Timing | Duration |
|---------|------|-----------|--------|----------|
| CJC-1295 (no DAC) | 100 mcg | Daily | Before bed (22:00) | 6 weeks |
| Ipamorelin | 100 mcg | Daily | Before bed (22:00) | 6 weeks |

**Rationale:** CJC-1295 stimulates growth hormone release; Ipamorelin is a selective GH secretagogue with minimal cortisol/prolactin elevation. Combined, they produce a synergistic GH pulse without the side effects of stronger secretagogues like GHRP-6.

### Why No DAC?

CJC-1295 with DAC creates a constant GH elevation (like HGH therapy), which can cause insulin resistance and water retention over time. CJC-1295 without DAC produces pulsatile GH release, which is more physiologic and safer for longer protocols.

### Lifestyle Protocol

| Area | Protocol |

## What Worked (and What Didn't)

### What Worked

1. **Sleep optimization** — The peptide sleep improvement + consistent bedtime was the foundation.
2. **Protein target** — 180g/day preserved muscle despite deficit.
3. **Daily steps** — 10,000/day added ~400 kcal expenditure without stress.
4. **Zero alcohol** — Removed empty calories, improved sleep, reduced cravings.
5. **Peptide stack** — CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin improved recovery, sleep, and likely contributed to lean retention.

### What Didn't Work (or Was Overrated)

1. **Daily weigh-ins** — Caused unnecessary stress. Weekly averages would have been sufficient.
2. **Too aggressive initial deficit** — 2,200 kcal was fine, but I started at 1,800 and felt terrible. Adjusted up.
3. **Supplements** — Omega-3 and Vitamin D are good, but the "fat burner" I tried (not peptide-related) did nothing.

### The Honest Truth

**The peptides helped, but they were not the primary driver.** The structured lifestyle changes — consistent sleep, tracked nutrition, regular training, zero alcohol — accounted for at least 70% of the results. The peptides accelerated recovery, improved sleep quality, and likely helped retain lean mass. But without the lifestyle foundation, they would have done little.

## Side Effects and Safety

### Reported Side Effects

| Side Effect | Severity | Duration | Management |
|-------------|----------|----------|------------|
| Water retention | Mild | Days 8–12 | Self-limiting, resolved |
| Injection site irritation | Very mild | Occasional | Rotated sites, no action needed |
| Increased hunger | Mild | Week 1 | Adjusted meal timing |
| Mild hand tingling | Very mild | Week 3 | Temporary, resolved |

### What I Monitored

- Daily: Weight, sleep, energy, mood
- Weekly: DEXA, training performance, body measurements
- Bi-weekly: Fasting glucose (home monitor)
- Start/end: Full blood panel

### Red Flags I Would Have Stopped For

- Persistent water retention beyond 2 weeks
- Elevated fasting glucose (>6.0 mmol/L)
- Joint pain or carpal tunnel symptoms
- Significant mood changes or anxiety
- Any sign of insulin resistance
