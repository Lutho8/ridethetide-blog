## What Is BPC-157?

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) is a synthetic pentadecapeptide derived from a protective protein found in human gastric juice. It has been extensively studied in animal models for its remarkable regenerative properties.

**Important:** BPC-157 is not approved by SAHPRA or the FDA for human use. This article is for research and educational purposes only.

---

## Mechanism of Action

BPC-157 works through several pathways:

### 1. Angiogenesis Promotion
BPC-157 stimulates the formation of new blood vessels, improving blood flow to damaged tissues. This is critical for healing tendons, ligaments, and muscle tissue [1].

### 2. Nitric Oxide Pathway Modulation
The peptide upregulates the nitric oxide system, which plays a key role in vasodilation and tissue repair [2].

### 3. Growth Factor Expression
BPC-157 increases expression of growth factors including VEGF, TGF-β, and PDGF, accelerating the healing cascade [3].

### 4. Gut-Brain Axis Protection
Uniquely among peptides, BPC-157 shows protective effects on both the gastrointestinal tract and the central nervous system, making it valuable for gut healing and neuroprotection [4].

## South African Context

### Regulatory Status
BPC-157 is **not scheduled** by SAHPRA but is **not approved for human use**. It falls into a regulatory grey area for research purposes.

### Sourcing Considerations
- Ensure supplier provides Certificate of Analysis (COA)
- Verify peptide purity ≥98%
- Check for endotoxin levels <0.01 EU/μg
- Store refrigerated (2–8°C) upon receipt

### Shipping & Customs
- Importing peptides for personal research is generally tolerated in small quantities

- Expect 7–14 day shipping from international suppliers

## Reconstitution Guide

### Materials Needed
- BPC-157 vial (typically 5 mg)
- Bacteriostatic water (BAC water)
- 1 mL insulin syringe
- Alcohol swabs

### Step-by-Step

1. **Clean** the vial top with an alcohol swab
2. **Draw** 2 mL BAC water into syringe
3. **Inject** BAC water slowly into BPC-157 vial (down the side, not directly on powder)
4. **Swirl** gently — do not shake (peptides are fragile)
5. **Store** refrigerated at 2–8°C
6. **Use** within 30 days of reconstitution

### Concentration
- 5 mg BPC-157 + 2 mL BAC water = **2.5 mg/mL**
- 500 mcg dose = **0.2 mL (20 units on insulin syringe)**

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is BPC-157 detectable in drug tests?**
Standard drug tests do not screen for peptides. However, WADA has banned BPC-157 for competitive athletes.

**Can I use BPC-157 for gut issues?**
Animal studies show strong gut healing effects. Human research is limited. Consult a gastroenterologist.

**How should I store BPC-157?**
Lyophilized (powder): frozen (-20°C) for long-term storage. Reconstituted: refrigerated (2–8°C), use within 30 days.
